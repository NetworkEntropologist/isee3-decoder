# XIII. ISEE-C Telemetry Digital Subcom Addressing Format

*Original page 17, Revision B, 4/20/77*

![Section XIII: Digital Subcom Addressing Format](../images/sec13-digital-subcom-addressing.png)

This is the 64-step digital subcom (minor frame word 59) annotated with the digital subplexer input (DSI<sub>n</sub>) each subcom word is read from. Several subcom words (26, 47, 56–58, 60) are marked as spares wired to DSI inputs.

Notes on the original:

1. A dark triangle indicates the word gate is generated and used.
2. A light triangle indicates the word gate is generated but not used.
